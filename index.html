About the Platform

What is Plus 2 Guru?

Plus 2 Guru is an educational resource website created to make Class 11 and Class 12 study materials easier to find, understand, and use. The platform brings commonly needed exam-preparation resources together in one organized place.

Our goal is simple: help students understand lessons, revise important concepts, practice exam-style questions, and use previous papers as part of a regular study routine. Resources are organized by purpose so students can move from learning to practice without unnecessary steps.

A practical study method

Don't only collect notes. Use them.

A useful study resource should lead to action. Start with the lesson, write down the important ideas in your own words, practice questions without looking at the answer, and then use a past paper to check whether you can apply what you learned.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who is Plus 2 Guru for?

Plus 2 Guru is intended primarily for Class 11 and Class 12 students in Nepal who want organized study and exam-preparation resources.

What resources are available?

The homepage links to study notes, lab practical resources, model questions, past papers, study guides, MCQ practice and chapter-based videos.

Can I use these resources with my textbook?

Yes. The resources are intended to support learning and revision. Students should use their official textbook and classroom instruction as their primary study references.

Is the website free?

Plus 2 Guru is designed as a free educational resource platform for students.
